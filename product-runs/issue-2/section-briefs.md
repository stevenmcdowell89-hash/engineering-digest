## Foreword (50–80 words, UK-framed thread)
Last month's story was new rails with no loyalty on them. This month the plumbing began to consolidate: UK retail media got a cross-retailer alliance, its first exchange and its first certified network; Shopify threw the agent checkout open to every merchant; and two Gulf banks wired rewards into their apps. Yet what counts as a reward kept splintering — into experiences, membership tiers and closed-loop points that share less and less with plain cashback.

## Trend 02 — The agent checkout opens to every merchant
- Kicker: "Trend · Agentic commerce". Closer: **investigate**.
- Standfirst (delta-led): last month the card networks specified who an agent may pay; this month Shopify opened the agent checkout itself — to every merchant — and the offer still isn't an object in it.
- Pieces:
  1. Shopify Spring '26 (17 Jun) — approval gate removed; agent profiles + public MCP endpoint; UCP (with Google) discovery→checkout; trust tiers unlock direct checkout. Source: Shopify.
  2. What's missing — the release names no loyalty/offer/discount/reward object; the one standard for agent-readable incentives (Talon.One UIP) hasn't moved since January. Sources: Shopify (UCP scope); Talon.One UIP (no in-window movement).
  3. Backdrop line — this builds on the 10 June networks trust-layer moves (Issue 1); the platform side is what moved this month.
- Investigate closer (roadmap 2): the agent surface now reaches millions of merchants; offers, points and rewards remain absent as first-class objects on it. Evidenced gap, delta-framed, no instruction. Sources: Shopify; Talon.One.

Both are the reader's exact product, deployed by prospects/competitors on the reader's sales map. Third-person; the reader draws the inference.

## Trend 04 — What a reward is, re-priced
- Kicker: "Trend · Reward mechanics". Closer: **why it matters**.
- Standfirst (movement-led): this month reward value pulled away from plain cashback in three directions at once — into experiences, membership tiers and closed-loop points.
- Pieces:
  1. Barclays summer switch (9 Jun) — headline Premier reward is a £600 experience (Michelin dining, hotels, entertainment), not cash; £200 cash for standard. UK. Source: MoneyfactsCompare.
  2. ING Spain (10 Jun) — rewards packaged into four paid tiers (Revolut/N26 playbook). *Spain.* Source: The Objective.
  3. PayPal Rewards (US, ~29 Jun) — ends cash-out, forces points into closed-loop redemption; active opt-in. *US.* Source: PayPal.
- Why-it-matters closer: three answers to "what is reward value" — experiences, membership, closed-loop points — each competing with a cashback/CLO proposition on a different axis. UK-anchored; others flagged.
