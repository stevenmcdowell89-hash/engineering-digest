## Trend 01 — The offer follows the wallet

- **Kicker:** Trend · Distribution
- **Standfirst angle:** The card-linked offer's assumed home is the bank app. This month it turned up somewhere else three times — a payments platform, anyone's wallet, and an airline's points programme with banks reduced to the issuing rail.
- **Statline:** ~6m Zilch customers / $3.3bn annual merchant flow / 200+ countries of MDES coverage
- **Feeding pieces:**
  1. *A competitor's platform ships inside a payments app* — Rezolve Ai × Zilch, 21 Jul. Reward's engagement and commerce-media platform embedded in Zilch's journey; hundreds of UK retailers; nearly 6m customers; $3.3bn a year to merchants; Reward in 15+ markets, $2bn cashback returned, $4bn targeted by 2030. Source: GlobeNewswire (Rezolve Ai).
  2. *Mastercard opens wallet-building to anyone — and names rewards as the reason* — Wallet Services, 15 Jul. SDKs, Secure Element applet, MDES tokenisation; no separate OS integrations or EMVCo certification; iOS first in US/UK/Europe; first consumer products before end-2026. Fourez quote on "benefits, rewards, discounts, points". Source: PYMNTS.
  3. *An airline takes the programme, the banks take the rail* — Riyadh Air RX Pay, late Jul, Saudi Arabia (flag region). Sfeer Points on everyday spend, now redeemable against any Mastercard purchase globally; multi-currency, split payments, instalments; phased from Saudi under Mastercard exclusivity. Source: Zawya (Riyadh Air).

## Trend 02 — The agent rails go live at the banks

- **Kicker:** Trend · Agentic commerce
- **Standfirst angle:** Lead with the delta. Issue 1: the networks specified who an agent may pay. Issue 2: Shopify opened the checkout. This month the reader's own bank customers started transacting — and the loyalty hook turned out to be identity, switched on almost nowhere.
- **Statline:** 30+ European banks live / 15 stores with identity capabilities / 0 of 11,414 exposing payment
- **Feeding pieces:**
  1. *Every major UK bank, live* — Visa, 3 Jul (state the date; it fell in Issue 2's window and was not covered). Agents browsing, selecting and buying within user-defined parameters; 30+ banks incl. Barclays, HSBC UK, Lloyds, Nationwide, NatWest, Revolut, Klarna; merchants lastminute.com, Frasers, Cleverbridge, BrickDepot; Payment Passkeys, Trusted Agent Protocol, Agent Directory; Cloudflare and Akamai. No offer/reward/loyalty object named. Source: FStech.
  2. *Identity becomes the transactability layer* — July protocol state. UCP Technical Council put saved payment methods on identity-linking infrastructure rather than a separate extension; identity linking is the capability that carries member and loyalty benefits. Adoption: 15 stores with identity, three more vendors declaring identity extensions, 99.7% on one spec revision, 0 of 11,414 exposing payment. Attribute to the tracker by name. Source: UCP Checker.
  3. *Nothing shipped on the incentive side* — independent tracker records no prominent loyalty-programme integrations and no dedicated discount protocols in the period; the two standards that would carry them are dated background (Talon.One UIP, 28 Jan; UCP identity linking, 19 Mar). Source: agenticplug.
- **Closer:** `.investigate` — evidenced gap, explicitly delta-framed: the gap moved from "no object" to "the object rides on identity, and almost nobody has turned it on". No instruction.

## Trend 03 — Everyone is selling the engagement layer

- **Kicker:** Trend · Competitive set
- **Standfirst angle:** In one month a card network, a martech platform and a loyalty specialist each pitched the same thing — personalised engagement, sold as a service — into the same bank and retailer budget the reader sells into.
- **Statline:** 34% VAS growth / 15% who find marketing "very relevant" / 5.8m members in months
- **Feeding pieces:**
  1. *The network sells engagement* — Visa Q3, call 28 Jul, reported 30 Jul. VAS +34% to $3.8bn, near a third of revenue, positioned as a loyalty and engagement engine; Brazilian bank World Cup promotion (1m participants, 8% activation lift, $400m incremental volume — flag Brazil); Mexican co-brand 5× average transaction size on match days (flag Mexico); Visa Direct behind DoorDash Crimson; white-label AI financial assistant for issuer apps. Source: The Wise Marketer. Use product substance only — no revenue-record framing.
  2. *The martech platform sells loyalty* — Dotdigital, 16 Jul. Customer Trends Index, 4,000 consumers across UK/US/Australia/Singapore: 62% points, 53% free shipping, 52% cashback, 45% raised spend for a tier, 53% say programmes matter to a future relationship, 15% find marketing "very relevant". Dotdigital Loyalty launched alongside. Source: PR Newswire.
  3. *The specialist sells scale* — Eagle Eye × Central Retail Vietnam, 27 Jul. AI-personalised programme past 5.8m members within months (flag Vietnam). Source: The Wise Marketer Loyalty Newswire.
