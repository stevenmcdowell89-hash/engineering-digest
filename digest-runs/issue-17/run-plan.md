## Format decision

**Standard Weekly.** The week offers three genuinely distinct, worth-reading anchors across three
operational concerns (Management & teams, Ways of working, Wider landscape/banking) plus a full
back-of-book (In Practice, Outside In, Leadership Read all earn their place). Phase 1 flagged a
possible "how AI agents will pay" deep dive (Open USD + Visa EU agentic payments + Mastercard Agent
Pay for Machines). **Rejected — no single story dominates.** The cluster is three parallel
announcements, not one event with 2–3 SVG-worthy data threads; a payments deep dive would also
consume the whole issue as a *Wider-landscape*-flavoured topic (breaking the breadth guardrail after
three landscape-heavy leads in four weeks), and two of its three legs are compromised — Mastercard
Agent Pay is barred (Issue 15 anchor) and the Visa primary URL 403s. `consecutive_non_deepdive` moves
to 9; a deep dive is due soon but not on this material. Keep the payments cluster as one QT (Open USD)
plus Radar/Other News, and track for a future deep dive when one leg dominates.

### Why this lead

The soft breadth guardrail (v3.4) applies hard this week: the last four leads ran
Wider-landscape / Ways-of-working / Wider-landscape / Wider-landscape. A fourth landscape-adjacent
lead would be indefensible when a stronger operational-concern story exists. The AI-washing layoffs
story is a **Management & teams** lead that breaks the streak cleanly: it is a genuine news event

combined so-what + narrative-tension (HIGH) + UK/ME-relevance (high) test, and it triggers the exact
leadership conversation the reader owns: if teams stop hiring graduates now, the "system verifier"
seniors of 2028 never get trained. **Lead sanity check (v3.1):** top-3 on the combined score,
comfortably. PASS.

Lead (people/hiring) → QT1 (AI-coding budget reality) → QT2 (payments rail the reader's bank
customers live on) walks the reader from the org, through delivery economics, out to the wider world
— brisk news up front, three concerns, three clean events. QT1 and the Lead share an AI substrate but
sit in different concerns and trigger different conversations (levelling/pipeline vs finance/metering).
QT2 is the non-AI counterweight in the anchor set.
